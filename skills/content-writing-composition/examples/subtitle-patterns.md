# Verbatim subtitle examples

Confirmed real subtitles, quoted verbatim, from a real author's published Medium pieces — used to calibrate the two subtitle shapes described in `../references/platform-metadata.md`. Request verbatim quotes the same way when calibrating against any other author's corpus; a paraphrased summary of "the subtitle style" is not useful for imitation.

> "When async tasks panic and runtimes die, your subprocess guard is the last code that runs. Make it count. (And watch out for ^C.)"

Shape (b): a plain statement of what happened ("your subprocess guard is the last code that runs"), then an imperative ("Make it count") followed by a parenthetical aside that undercuts it slightly.

> "Building a fail-open deduplication cache in Rust with batched writes, exponential backoff, and graceful degradation."

Shape (a): a comma-separated list of the concrete techniques in the piece (batched writes, exponential backoff, graceful degradation), framed by what was built and in what language.

> "Recursive diff chunking, token-aware compression, and why solving your own friction beats general-purpose mediocrity."

Shape (a) again, but closed with the wry "why X beats Y" thesis clause instead of trailing off after the technique list — this is the more common closing move for shape (a) once a real thesis exists to state.
